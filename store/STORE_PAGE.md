## Full description

**The swarm is coming. The beat tells you when to move.**

VOIDBLOOM is a fast, neon survivors-like with one big twist: the music is the game clock. Your weapons fire on their own. Your job is to fly, and to dash on the beat.

**Dash on the beat**
A ring closes in on your ship and lands on every beat. Dash as it lands for a Perfect: you can't be hit, and you cut through everything in your path. Chain Perfects to climb from Groove ×2 to Overdrive for up to 40% more damage, while the synth score builds with you. Miss, and the Groove slips away.

**Build something ridiculous**
- 11 weapons, each with an evolved form
- 16 passive upgrades, rare double-level drops and epic boons
- Reroll and banish to shape your build

**Then make a deal with the void**
When your build is full, the game doesn't run out of things to offer. Void Pacts give unlimited power, and every pact raises the Heat: more enemies, tougher and faster. How hot can you run it?

**Features**
- A 15-sector campaign with three stars to earn in every sector
- Pilot Rank with a reward at every rank: ships, paints, perks and shards
- Rotating contracts, and double XP on your first run each day
- Beat Sync: dashes, shockwaves and the soundtrack all share one clock
- Solo or local two-player co-op on one screen
- 5 ships, 3 arenas with their own hazards, 3 bosses
- Nightmare mode and Endless mode once you win
- The Hangar: permanent upgrades and unlockable ships, earned by playing
- 17 achievements
- Keyboard, controller and touch
- Every sprite is drawn in code and every sound is synthesized as you play

**A run takes 15 minutes. You will want another one.**

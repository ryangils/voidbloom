HOW TO PLAY

Fly

Move with WASD, the arrow keys or a controller stick.Drag anywhere on the screen to fly. Your weapons fire on their own. Collect the crystals enemies drop to level up.

Dash on the beat

Press Space (or A on a controller)Tap with a second finger to dash. A ring closes in on your ship and lands on the beat of the music. Dash as it lands for a Perfect: you can't be hit, and you slash through everything in your path.

Build your Groove

Chain Perfects to climb from Groove ×2 to Overdrive for up to 40% more damage. Miss the beat, or stop dashing for two bars, and you drop a tier. Every bar, a shockwave pulses out from your ship.

Level up

Each level offers three upgrades. Max a weapon to evolve it. Once your build is full, Void Pacts offer unlimited power, and each one raises the Heat: more enemies, tougher and faster.

Last the run

Bosses arrive at 5:00, 10:00 and 15:00. From 8:00, Void Rot weakens healing. Every run earns void shards to spend in the Hangar on permanent upgrades and new ships.

Co-op

Player 2 flies with the arrow keys and dashes with Enter, or uses a second controller.Player 1 uses the left half of the screen and player 2 the right half. A downed pilot comes back on a timer, or straight away when you level up.
